# Hot Seat roadmap

This is the long list of Hot Seats we could make: 113 candidates, so you can cut to 100. Production follows the order of this table, one Hot Seat at a time. Deep research happens later, when a row enters production.

**Columns**

- **id**: the folder name it will get (`content/hotseats/<id>/`).
- **Turning point**: the big call the player makes as CEO.
- **Year(s)**: when it happened.
- **Indian**: `yes` if the story plays out in India (55 of 113 rows, 49%).
- **Risk flags**: see "Flags explained" at the end. Litigation was checked with a quick web search on 27 Oct 2026; what we found is noted in the cell.
- **Status**: `ok` means no flag. `needs your call` means a flag is set and you decide if we make it.

**How to approve**

1. Delete the rows you don't want (or write `cut` in Status).
2. For each `needs your call` row, change Status to `ok` or `cut`.
3. Move rows up or down to change the order.

Hook and complexity are filled in when a row enters production.

## Flags explained

- **active litigation**: a court or regulator case about this story may still be open. We'd need to be extra careful with facts and wording, and a lawyer may want to see the script. A quick web search was done for each one; check again before production.
- **living people central**: the story turns on what one named living person did. We never invent quotes, and we must stick to the public record about them. Founders who simply made a business call are not flagged.
- **thin sources**: few solid sources are easy to find, so the real history may be hard to tell well. Deep research may fix this, or the row gets cut.

## Summary
41 claims checked: 36 supported, 2 unsupported, 3 contradicted.
- Contradicted: (1) "India opened its skies to private airlines in 2004" — it opened the market to more competition; private airlines already existed. (2) "Airbus is getting ready to launch the A320neo" at end of 2010 — it had already launched it in December 2010 (both scene2010 variants). (3) Kingfisher "lost its licence" in October 2012 (legend and realHistory) — the licence was suspended.
- Unsupported: "the economy grew 6.9% last year" (a forecast in the source) and "tickets for as little as Rs 500" (an analyst's remark, not a reported fare).
- Focus items: the 2005 deal is called a firm order by Wikipedia and a book excerpt in search results; no source calls it a letter of intent, and "ordered" is safe. The on-time figures (78.4% vs 74.5%, Nov 2009) and FY2008/09 figures (+$17.6M; Kingfisher −$342.7M, Jet −$86.1M, SpiceJet −$75.4M) match CAPA exactly. FY2012-13 Rs 7.87bn sixfold matches Reuters. Kingfisher comparisons name only the company. Co-founders are not named or characterised.
- Alternate paths: all "Our estimate:" text is ok; minor path-logic wording in d5 ("180 more A320s") on non-A320 paths.

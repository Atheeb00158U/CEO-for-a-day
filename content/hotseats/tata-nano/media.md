**Status: made (2026-11-05).** All 25 images are in `media/`, generated with ChatGPT in the browser on Atheeb's sign-in (one chat, so the car looks the same in every picture), then prepared with `npm run media`. Each was checked for logos, readable brand names, readable text and famous faces. The first newspaper picture (tn_d1_cheap) showed a garbled, wrong price, so it was redone with no digits.

**Archives searched** (see `research.md`, Archive footage): movingimagearchive.com has nothing Indian or from 2008–2011 (closest, "Robot Reality" (1990), car-factory robots, is not period-correct). No film is referred to, so FILMGRAB doesn't apply. Frame Set and Fashion Gone Rogue have nothing that fits. News footage of the 2008 unveiling and of Singur exists but is copyrighted. So every image is planned as **generated** (ChatGPT in the browser, on Atheeb's sign-in), using the prompts in `image_list.md`.

Rules for every image: India, 2008–2010, period-correct. No logos, badges or readable brand names. **No real person's likeness**: no chairman, no politician, no recognisable face at the unveiling. The car is a tiny, tall, rounded four-seat hatchback with no badge. No Singur protest or any political image (the land case is still in court); the Gujarat plant appears only as a generic factory.

## Other issues

- Living people: no names in hotseat.json (grep for the 1996–97 CEO, Microsoft's co-founder, Be's founder and the CFO found nothing). They appear only by role ("The CEO who bought NeXT", "The money chief", "Be, a young company"). No motives or blame are attributed to them. The only name anywhere is in a source URL slug ("steve-jobss-seven-key-decisions"), and the co-founder is deceased; the source label already uses "The co-founder's". ok.
- Anachronism: d1 is set in "late 1996" but treats Copland as an ongoing, late project; Apple had cancelled it in August 1996 [19]. See the contradicted claim above.
- Overstated wording: hook says "Your company is losing a billion dollars" while the opening scene is late 1996 with an $816M loss; the $1B loss was FY1997. Suggest "nearly a billion dollars". Minor: "its web browser became the Mac's default" (future commitment in [5]); "took $150 million from Microsoft" in realHistory (it was a share sale); "$152 million" includes a one-off ARM share gain.
- Path logic: scene1998 real variant requires next+peace+grid and mentions only NeXT and clone write-offs: consistent. Legend real variant requires all five real flags and mentions iMacs: consistent. scene1997 "next" variant only mentions NeXT: consistent. d3 "next" variant mentions the returning co-founder only when "next" was chosen: consistent. ## Summary
48 claims checked: 46 supported, 2 contradicted, 0 unsupported. Alternate text is all marked "Our estimate:"; no quotes; no living people named. All six focus items hold: NeXT $400M (Apple's own releases; Low End Mac's $430M is the outlier), the Microsoft terms ($150M of non-voting shares, patent cross-licence, future Office for Mac, IE as default), the four-box grid and end of clones (with the Power Computing $10M + $100M buyout), the Think different debut on 28 Sept 1997, the iMac (6 May / 15 Aug 1998, 519,000 in the Dec 1998 quarter), and $309M in FY1998 as the first profitable year since 1995.
Fixes needed: (1) d1.setup/intro: Copland was officially cancelled in August 1996, so in "late 1996" it is not just "badly late". Reword it as shelved or failed, or move the scene to mid-1996. (2) d2.setup: Microsoft made Mac software from 1984 (Multiplan at the Mac's launch), not "since 1985". Optional tightening: hook "nearly a billion dollars"; "would become the default browser"; realHistory "sold Microsoft $150 million of shares"; note that the $152M Dec 1998 quarter profit includes a $29M ARM share gain.

## Legal wording
- realHistory gives the 1993 Prime Minister claim with the inquiry finding but not the denial. Add "Rao denied it" (or "the Prime Minister denied it") [7]. The d5 real outcome already has both.
- realHistory ("He bought with bank money routed through his firm") and nodes.d3.options.nhb.outcome ("using money from the National Housing Bank, raised the same way") state the NHB/SBI diversion as fact. The criminal NHB case against him abated in April 2002 with no verdict [6]. The facts come from the RBI committee and [1], not from a court. Attribute them: "An RBI committee found he bought with bank money..." and "...using money from the National Housing Bank, raised the same way, an RBI committee later found." Keep the word "convicted" for the Maruti case only, as the text does now.
- The hook "made the market soar" gives him the whole rally, which is an unproven manipulation claim. Reword as above.
- The death is given only as "in custody", with no cause and no foul-play rumour. Good.

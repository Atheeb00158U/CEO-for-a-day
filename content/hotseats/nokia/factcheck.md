## Summary
62 claims checked: 51 supported, 10 unsupported, 1 contradicted. All real-flagged outcomes, the survivor ending and realHistory are substantially supported. 3 alternate-branch scene/setup variants state invented events as fact without an estimate marker.
Top 3 fixes:
1. d2.setup: "Windows Phone, which has only 3.4%" is wrong. The 3.4% is Windows Phone plus Windows Mobile in Q4 2010 [3]. Say "Microsoft's phone software".
2. d1.options.symbian.outcome: "now fully controlled the most popular smartphone software" is misleading, since Symbian was being opened up through the Symbian Foundation [2]. Also d2.windows.outcome: Lumia 800 and 710 launched together [9], so do not call the 800 alone "the first".
3. Mark or rephrase the alternate-branch variants (scene2010 text[0], scene2013 text[0], d5 setup[0]) as estimates. Soften the intro's India claim ("almost everyone you meet"), which no source supports.

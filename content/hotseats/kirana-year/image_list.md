# Images: kirana-year

Made-up story, set today (2020s) in a mid-sized Madhya Pradesh town. One small family kirana: one room, steel shelves to the ceiling, a QR code card at the counter, sacks of loose grain, glass jars of namkeen. No logos or readable brand names on any pack, board or app; no real person's likeness. Asha is 27, Baba is about 60 (made-up people; keep them looking the same in every image).

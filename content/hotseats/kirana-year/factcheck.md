## Story numbers (made up; checked for scale only, not facts)
- Sales ₹10,000 a day (about ₹3 lakh a month, ₹36 lakh a year): a one-room shop with ~1,000 items [2]; keeps under the ₹40 lakh GST registration line in research.md. No GST claim appears in the file.
- Udhaar ₹40,000 → ₹60,000; fund ₹60,000 ≈ three slow months; rain stock ₹25,000; wedding ask ₹50,000; forty WhatsApp orders: all invented, proportionate to the sales above.
- "The wholesaler wants his money within two weeks": no source we could open on distributor credit to kiranas (IIMB study blocked by bot protection; BloombergQuint host down). A fintech blog (vayana.com, "last mile finance") says retailer credit periods run 7 to 45 days. Two weeks is within that; it is one wholesaler's demand in the story, not a stated rule.
- Off-path outcomes (wholesaler moves to cash first, a trader buys stock at cost, interest eats the Diwali profit, losses at app prices) are story events for a made-up shop, not claims about real people or firms.

## Real shop or person?
- No GST, UPI-company, brand or quick-commerce company is named in the story text ("the app", "a store that delivers in ten minutes"). Paytm, PhonePe etc. appear only inside source [9].

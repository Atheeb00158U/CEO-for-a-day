## Other issues
- Quotes: none from real people. Sardar Patel's advice is a paraphrase without quotation marks and matches [1]. "Purely the Best" is a quoted slogan, not a person's words.
- Living people: none named (Patel, Nehru, Prasad, Shastri, Dalaya and daCunha are dead, and most are unnamed in the text).
- Anachronisms: none found.
- Overstated or loose wording:
  - realHistory "From 1965 the National Dairy Development Board copied the model across India through Operation Flood": suggested rewrite "The National Dairy Development Board, set up in 1965, spread the model across India through Operation Flood, which began in 1970."
  - realHistory "In 1966 the cheeky Amul girl ads began": [13] supports a first topical ad in March 1966, but [15] says the idea was conceived in 1967. Consider "In 1966 Amul hired a new ad agency, and the cheeky Amul girl ads soon followed."
  - The 31 October 1955 opening date rests on the co-operative's own page [1]; [11] gives 1956. Consider "In October 1955" or keep it with [1] as the source, knowing the date is disputed by at least one secondary source.
  - "the world's first spray-dryer for buffalo milk" / "a first in the world" rests on Amul's own pages [1][2] and Wikipedia [6], which appears to derive from them. Consider "the first ... in the world, the co-operative says" or "a breakthrough experts had said was impossible" ([4]).
  - The strike outcome's "the Bombay Milk Scheme almost collapsed" is only in [1]; [3] confirms only that the strike succeeded.
  - scene1970.text[0]: "have built milk unions just like yours" by 1970 is not dated by any opened source for Baroda and Surat (see Claims).
- Path logic: no problems found. d3.setup[0] requires both `strike` and `plant`; scene1970.text[0] requires `national`; legend.text[0] requires all five real flags. d5.setup[1] (the real text) is the default for every player except `closed`, and it is generic enough to fit every path.

## Summary
48 claims checked: 47 supported (several with caveats) and 1 unsupported (that the Baroda and Surat unions existed by 1970). None contradicted. The 15-day strike, the 14 Dec 1946 registration, the 1948 figures and the 1964 Shastri visit are independently confirmed by the President of India's 2015 speech [3]. The 1965 founding of NDDB is confirmed by NDDB [7] and Kurien's 1988 lecture [9]. GCMMF in 1973 is confirmed by [4] and [18]. Operation Flood details are from [5].

Most important fixes:
1. realHistory: separate NDDB (1965) from Operation Flood (1970).
2. Soften or attribute the 31 Oct 1955 opening date ([11] says 1956) and the "world first" claim, which rests on Amul's own pages.
3. realHistory: "In 1966 the Amul girl ads began" conflicts with [15] (conceived 1967). Hedge it or rely on [13].
4. scene1970.text[0]: hedge or verify that all five district unions existed by 1970.
5. Optional: soften the past-tense `why` lines on alternate options.

## Claims
- [supported] 1982 joint company of the Tata group's Forbes company and Sweden's Electrolux (nodes.intro.text, realHistory) — [1] founded 15 Feb 1982, 60:40 JV of Tata Group's Forbes & Company and Electrolux; [3] JV of Electrolux and Forbes Gokak, Tata holding a stake
- [supported] Based in Bombay (nodes.intro.text, nodes.d1.options.homes.outcome) — [1] HQ Mumbai; [2] "a single office in Mumbai"
- [supported] First product was the Euroclean vacuum cleaner (nodes.intro.text, realHistory) — [5] began in 1982 "introducing a range of vacuum cleaners (EuroClean)"; [3] early on "only sold vacuum cleaners"; [7] first product (1982) a vacuum cleaner
- [supported] Most Indian homes had never used one and swept with a broom (nodes.intro.text) — [7] homes relied on brooming, mopping or domestic help, which kept vacuum adoption low; "one of the country's first vacuum cleaners"
- [supported] Sold in homes, not shops; salespeople showed the machine working (nodes.d1.options.homes.label/outcome, realHistory) — [3] in-home demonstrations by salespeople; [6] they "demonstrated a vacuum cleaner or a purifier, and sold on the spot"
- [supported] By 1987 one Bombay office and 20 salespeople; the company was struggling (nodes.d1.options.homes.outcome, realHistory) — [2] "a single office in Mumbai with 20 sales representatives", "floundering firm"
- [supported] Aquaguard added in the 1980s, India's first water purifier; few homes had used one (nodes.s1988.text default, realHistory) — [5] Aquaguard 1984, "the first ever WP player in the country". Launch year varies by source; the story says only "late 1980s"/"1980s"
- [supported] Kept a premium price and talked families out of boiling water (nodes.d2.setup, nodes.d2.options.compare.label/outcome, realHistory) — [3] aim to "persuade Indians to stop boiling their otherwise undrinkable tap water"; $175 Aquaguard (premium). The fuel/time/illness pitch in "why" is teaching framing, not a sourced script
- [supported] Three of every four purifiers sold were Aquaguard in 2004–05 (nodes.s2005.text compare variant, realHistory) — [2] peak 75% in 2004; [3] 75% share (2005)
- [supported] 1990s: each machine fitted to a tap, filters wear out, salespeople paid mostly on new sales (nodes.d3.setup default) — [3] company plumber installs each purifier, pay 60% commission; [5] filter/candle changes charged separately
- [supported] Company plumbers fit, technicians return to service, water-testing labs, yearly service plans (nodes.d3.options.service.outcome, realHistory) — [3] plumber installs, 15 Aquacheck labs; [5] annual servicing about Rs 3k a year
- [supported] Families keep buying filters and plans; service a big, steady income (nodes.d3.options.service.outcome, realHistory) — [7] FY23 service (AMCs, filters, spares) nearly 30% of sales, "recurring annuity income"; [5] filter changes chargeable
- [supported] 2005: about 5,300 young salespeople, aged 26 on average; most pay from each sale; training and motorbikes (nodes.s2005.text compare variant) — [3] 5,300-person sales force, average age 26, three weeks' training, 60% commission, motorcycles after about six months or 60 units
- [supported] 2012: close to 9 million customer homes (nodes.d4.setup compare variant) — [2] "close to 9 million homes"
- [supported] Share fell from three in four to about six in ten by 2012 (nodes.d4.setup, realHistory) — [2] 75% (2004) to 60% (2012), as competitors entered
- [supported] Rivals sell in shops (nodes.d4.setup, nodes.d4.options.online.outcome, nodes.survivor.text) — [4] HUL and Kent "far more visible at retail counters"
- [supported] Gated buildings make home visits harder (nodes.d4.setup, realHistory) — [4] gated communities made cold calling less effective
- [supported] About 7,500 people visiting homes in 2012 (nodes.d4.setup compare variant) — [2] "7,500-strong sales force", over 60 million doors a year
- [supported] Kept the sales force as the core; online ads bring demo requests (nodes.d4.options.online.label/outcome) — [4] ruled out disbanding, Eurochamps are "my core", digital marketing to get demo requests; [2] tens of thousands of web leads a month
- [supported] More than 8,000 salespeople in 2014; retail not a focus while rivals sell in shops (nodes.d4.options.online.outcome, realHistory) — [4] "more than 8,000 Eurochamps"; "Retail sales is still not a big focus area"
- [supported] About 25 years building a trusted name, then rivals grew through shops (nodes.survivor.text first variant) — [3] 75% share 2005; [6] Aquaguard became the generic word for a purifier; [2], [4] rivals and retail
- [supported] Company later sold; new owners since 2022 sell in shops and online too; by 2025 door sales about a quarter (nodes.survivor.text first variant, realHistory) — [6] Advent agreed in 2021, took 72.56% after the 1 Feb 2022 demerger; CFO (Nov 2025): four channels (direct, general trade, modern trade, e-commerce) within 3–4 points of each other, so direct is about 25%. [1] is inconsistent on 2021/2022; "since 2022" follows [6]

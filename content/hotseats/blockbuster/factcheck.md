## Other issues
- Quotes attributed to real people: none. The "laughed us out of the room" anecdote and the CEO's "dot-com hysteria" remark from [1] are correctly left out, and no real people are named.
- Blockbuster's private thinking in 2000 (see claims): the d1 "no" outcome hedges properly ("according to one of its founders"). The real option's label, "why" ("Judging a startup by today's profit…") and takeaway ("you judged a startup by its losses") still state Blockbuster's reasoning as fact, and the only source is the Netflix side.
- Continuity: the role is "You run Blockbuster, 2000 to 2008", yet scene2007 says "a new CEO takes over. That's you." The player is both the old and the new CEO, and the real 2007 decision was made by a different person from the 2000–2006 decisions. Consider "You stay on as CEO" or a note that the player now steps into the new CEO's seat.
- d1 "no" why: "Saying no cost you four years" is a causal claim not supported as fact. Blockbuster could have built a service without buying Netflix. Soften to "you then waited four years to go online."
- Anachronisms: none found. DVD-by-mail in 2000, downloads in 2007 and Total Access in 2006 are all period-correct.

## Summary
45 claims checked: 39 supported, 4 unsupported and 2 contradicted. The unsupported ones are "biggest name in movie rental" in 2000, "your stores make a lot of money" in 2000, Blockbuster judging Netflix "by its losses", and "customers liked" the end of late fees. The contradicted ones are realHistory "online service only in 2004" and d4 "tried new store formats and sold more electronics". All 8 alternate-history options and both estimate endings start with "Our estimate:". Two alternate-branch scene variants mix invented and real facts without marking them. There are no quotes attributed to real people.

Top 3 fixes:
1. d1 "no" option: remove the framing that Blockbuster judged Netflix by its losses (label, why, takeaway). The only evidence is a Netflix founder's account of a "niche" remark, so attribute it the way the outcome does.
2. realHistory: change "its own online service only in 2004" to "its own online DVD rental service only in 2004". Blockbuster had blockbuster.com and a video-on-demand trial in 2000 [17].
3. d4 "charge" outcome: change "tried new store formats and sold more electronics" to "planned tests of new store layouts and of selling small electronics" [10]. Also drop "Customers liked it" from scene2006, or attribute it.

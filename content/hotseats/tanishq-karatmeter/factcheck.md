## Summary
49 claims checked: 45 supported, 2 unsupported, 2 contradicted. The Karatmeter year of 1997 is solid (book [1] and its Rediff excerpt [2]). Other dates found were 1998 (an agency blog) and post-2002 (Simplanations, which is wrong). No opened source gives 1999. The Rs 399 range, the 2000 crisis details and the first profit of Rs 2.07 crore on Rs 188 crore are all confirmed by Rediff 2003. Impure to Pure is dated to 14 May 2002 by a contemporary Rediff/Business Standard report. That report confirms the 19-karat threshold and that Tanishq bore the gold cost ("at no extra cost").

Fixes needed:
1. "In May a new managing director takes over" (scene2000, all variants): the successor was only named in May 2000 and took charge on 31 March 2002. Reword to "the board names the next managing director".
2. realHistory "launched in India in 1996": change to "opened its first store in 1996". The brand launched in 1995 through other outlets.
3. "free" testing (d3 label/outcome, d5.stoptest label, realHistory) rests only on weak secondary sources. Soften it, or accept the 1998 agency blog as the source.
4. "paid only the making charges" (d5.swap) is secondary-only. The primary source says "at no extra cost". Consider "at no extra cost for the gold".
5. Optional: gate the 2002–03 figures in d5.swap and the "fifth-anniversary" wording to the real path.

No specific jewellers are defamed and no living people are named.
